The experience

Big Fish Reboot

Panguitch is built around one thing: trout. You can troll open water for cruising fish, work shorelines and structure, or target tiger trout and cutthroat with more aggressive presentations. The lake’s high elevation and open basin make changing weather and conditions part of the experience.

Why fish Panguitch Lake

This is where you go when the goal is not simply catching trout but looking for a genuinely large Southern Utah trout.

Good to know

Questions about Panguitch Lake

Does Henrie Outfitters guide fishing trips at Panguitch Lake?

Yes. Henrie Outfitters LLC, owned and guided by James Henrie, runs guided fishing trips at Panguitch Lake and at other lakes across Southern Utah.

What fish can I catch at Panguitch Lake?

Panguitch Lake has rainbow trout, cutthroat trout and tiger trout.

What is included on a guided fishing trip at Panguitch Lake?

A private full day charter for just your group: up to 6 hours of fishing (about 8 hours with travel, depending on where you are coming from), your boat and captain, all rods, tackle, bait and lures, Coast Guard approved life jackets, bottled water and ice, fish cleaned and bagged with ice if you want to keep them, and expert instruction and local knowledge.
