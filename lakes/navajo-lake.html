The experience

Lava Tube Trout Lake

Navajo is almost the opposite of Lake Powell. You are fishing at more than 9,000 feet, surrounded by spruce and pine rather than desert cliffs. The water is cold, quiet and surprisingly deep in places despite the lake’s relatively small size. It feels like a remote mountain trout expedition.

Why fish Navajo Lake

Brook trout, rainbow trout and a completely different high-country fishing experience. The surrounding forest and elevation make the trip feel more like Colorado or Montana than Southern Utah.

Guided by James Henrie, owner of Henrie Outfitters LLC. Call (435) 592-0680 or email henrieoutfitters@gmail.com to plan a trip.
