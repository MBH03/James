# Owner checklists

## Google Business Profile
1. Sign in with the owner's Google account at business.google.com and add the business.
2. Name: the real business name only (no keywords added).
3. Category: choose the real primary category (for example Fishing charter or Fishing camp) and add real secondary ones.
4. Business type: if customers meet at lakes or job sites rather than a shop, set it up as a service-area business, hide the home address if the owner prefers, and list the real areas served.
5. Phone, website (the live domain) and hours. Add the services actually offered with a short description and, if confirmed, the price.
6. Photos: real photos of the owner, equipment and results. Add more over time.
7. Verify (video, phone, or postcard; it can take days).
8. Reviews: ask customers after the trip with a short link or QR code to the review form. Do not tell them what to write, do not offer rewards, and reply to every review.
9. Posts: occasional real updates and photos.

## Search Console
1. Add the property for the live domain (domain property with a DNS TXT record, or URL-prefix with an HTML file in the repo).
2. Submit `https://<domain>/sitemap.xml` under Sitemaps.
3. Use URL Inspection on the home page and each key page; click "Request indexing" for new pages.
4. Check Indexing, then Pages: read each reason. "Alternate page with proper canonical tag" for `?param=` URLs and redirected old addresses is normal.
5. Check Performance after 2 to 4 weeks: queries, pages, impressions, clicks, average position. Compare the same date range before and after a change.
6. Check Enhancements for structured-data warnings.

## Outside signals
- Real local links: the chamber of commerce, tourism boards, lodging partners, the licensing body's guide directory, local news.
- Consistent business name, address and phone on every listing.
