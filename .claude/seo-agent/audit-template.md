# SEO audit template

**Search being audited:** query, location, device, date of the results seen.
**Evidence used:** screenshots, exports, pages read, repo files. List what was NOT available.

## 1. What the results page looks like
Who ranks and why (place in name, address, price in snippet, directory or marketplace, social page, government page). Is there a map pack? Is there an AI overview and what does it cite?

## 2. Is the site indexed and healthy?
Domain live, canonical, title, H1, meta, sitemap, robots, schema validity, mobile layout, speed notes. Search Console status of the key pages, with each "not indexed" reason marked harmless or real.

## 3. Gaps compared with what ranks
Real, structural, and fixable-by-page-edit gaps, each labelled.

## 4. Fix now (repo)
Numbered, each with the file or page and the exact change. Mark anything that needs a fact from the owner.

## 5. Owner tasks (Google tools and outside)
Numbered steps with who, where, and rough time.

## 6. Time, links and reviews
What only time or outside signals will change. No ranking promises.

## 7. Advice from outside sources, checked
Table: claim, verified or not, source, action.

## 8. How to measure
Which Search Console report, which metric, when to look again (weeks, not days).
