# Owner intake questions
Send only the questions that are still unknown. Short answers or a voice memo are fine. Record every answer in the build data. Anything left blank stays off the site.

## 1. The business
1. Legal business name and city. Phone, email, does the owner take texts?
2. Which towns does the business serve and meet customers in?
3. Licenses, insurance and permits, in the exact wording the owner uses.
4. Other guides or staff, or always the owner?
5. Years of experience, stated in the owner's own number.

## 2. Pricing and booking
6. Prices for each group size. Per person or per unit (boat, hour, job)?
7. Half-day or other options and their prices.
8. Maximum group size and the cost of each extra person.
9. Deposit amount, payment methods, cancellation and weather or reschedule policy.
10. How far ahead should customers book? Which months fill first?
11. Show prices on the site, or "call for rates"?

## 3. What a service is like
12. Start and end times. Where do customers meet?
13. What is included? What should customers bring or leave at home?
14. Licenses or permits customers must buy themselves, and who buys them.
15. Minimum age, kids, beginners, limited mobility, pets.
16. Private, group, corporate or party bookings?
17. Equipment (boat, vehicle, gear) in the owner's words.
18. A "perfect day" in a sentence or two.

## 4. Each destination or service (repeat per item)
19. What is it best for, and when (months or seasons)?
20. Access: where to start, fees, parking or launch details.
21. What makes it special in the owner's words? Who is it best for?
22. Rules or limits customers should know.

## 5. About the owner
23. Short bio in the owner's own words and a story or two.
24. What does the owner want customers to leave with?
25. Which social links should appear?

## 6. Proof
26. Google review count and rating (real numbers only) and 3 to 5 real quotes with permission.
27. Press, awards, partnerships.

## 7. Photos and video (ask for full-size originals)
- Hero: customer with a catch, result or product, the equipment on the job.
- Each destination or service: one wide shot and one detail shot.
- People: owner with customers, a portrait.
- Close-ups, equipment, results.
- 10 to 20 second video clip if available.
- Customers recognisable in a photo need their OK.
