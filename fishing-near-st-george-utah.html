Three lakes, three different days

St. George is the hub for Southern Utah's red rock bass fishing. Sand Hollow, Quail Creek and Gunlock are close to town, and each one is a different experience. This page helps you choose.

Sand Hollow Reservoir

Red Rock Bass Factory

Choose Sand Hollow if you want numbers of largemouth bass in red rock scenery, with a real chance at a big fish. It offers year round bass fishing.

  • Bluegill

Quail Creek Reservoir

Record Factory

Choose Quail Creek for big largemouth in clear water, with stocked rainbow trout in the mix. It has good facilities and boat ramps.

Gunlock Reservoir

Comeback Waterfall Bass

Choose Gunlock if you want smaller, quieter water where you can slow down and learn where the bass hold. When conditions are right, Gunlock Falls spills over the dam.

Staying in St. George for Zion?

Many visitors base themselves in St. George for Zion National Park. A guided fishing day on one of these lakes is an easy way to take a break from hiking. See fishing near Zion National Park.

A guided trip with Henrie Outfitters is a private full day charter with owner and guide James Henrie: up to 6 hours of fishing, so plan on about 8 hours with travel, depending on where you are coming from. Your boat, rods, tackle, bait and lures, life jackets, bottled water and ice are included, and fish are cleaned and bagged with ice if you want to keep them. Beginners, families and experienced anglers are all welcome.

Questions about fishing near St. George

Which lake near St. George is best for bass?

Sand Hollow Reservoir is the one to start with for numbers of largemouth bass. Quail Creek Reservoir can produce big largemouth in clear water, and Gunlock Reservoir is a smaller, quieter option.

Can I fish near St. George in winter?

Sand Hollow offers year round bass fishing. Contact us to ask about winter trips at the other lakes.

Do I need fishing experience?

No. Trips suit beginners, families and experienced anglers, and expert instruction is included.
