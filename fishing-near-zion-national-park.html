Fishing outside the park

Zion National Park is known for canyon hiking, not fishing. The guided fishing trips on this page are on lakes in the region around the park, not inside it. If you are planning a Zion trip, a guided fishing day gives you a slower day on the water between hikes. Check the National Park Service website for current rules about fishing inside the park.

A fishing day between park days

After days of hiking and shuttle lines, a guided fishing day is a different pace: a boat, a lake, and someone else doing the planning. You can start with no experience and still catch fish, and the day is built around your group.

A guided trip with Henrie Outfitters is a full 8 hour day on the water with owner and guide James Henrie. All fishing gear and equipment and lunch are provided, start and end times are customized to fit your schedule, and you get expert instruction and local knowledge. Beginners, families and experienced anglers are all welcome.

Questions about fishing near Zion

Can you fish inside Zion National Park?

This page covers guided trips on lakes near the park, not fishing inside it. Check the National Park Service website for current rules inside Zion.

Which lake is best for a Zion visitor?

For bass and red rock scenery, start with Sand Hollow Reservoir. Gunlock Reservoir is smaller and quieter, and Quail Creek Reservoir offers bass, crappie, bluegill and rainbow trout.

Do I need fishing experience?

No. Trips suit beginners, families and experienced anglers, and expert instruction is included.
