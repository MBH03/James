A day on the water between park days

Utah's five national parks are Zion, Bryce Canyon, Capitol Reef, Arches and Canyonlands. A road trip through them is a lot of hiking, driving and viewpoints. A guided fishing day gives you a slower, different kind of day: a lake, a boat, and a local guide doing the planning.

A guided trip with Henrie Outfitters is a private full day charter with owner and guide James Henrie: up to 6 hours of fishing, so plan on about 8 hours with travel, depending on where you are coming from. Your boat, rods, tackle, bait and lures, life jackets, bottled water and ice are included, and fish are cleaned and bagged with ice if you want to keep them. Beginners, families and experienced anglers are all welcome.

The fishing is on lakes in the region, outside the parks. Check the National Park Service for the rules inside each park.

Questions about fishing on a parks trip

Is there fishing inside Utah's national parks?

This page covers guided trips on lakes in the region, outside the parks. Check the National Park Service for current rules about fishing inside each park.

Which national parks can I pair with a fishing day?

Zion, Bryce Canyon and Capitol Reef each have lakes in the region that fit a guided fishing day. Arches and Canyonlands are in the Moab area, a long way from our lakes.

Do I need fishing experience?

No. Trips suit beginners, families and experienced anglers, and expert instruction is included.

How do I book a fishing day?

Call or text (435) 592-0680, email henrieoutfitters@gmail.com, or use the booking form. Customized start and end times are available.
