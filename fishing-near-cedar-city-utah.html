Questions about fishing near Cedar City

What kind of fishing is near Cedar City?

Mountain trout fishing. Panguitch Lake offers rainbow, cutthroat and tiger trout.

Is there bass fishing near Cedar City?

Our bass lakes are on the St. George side of Southern Utah: Sand Hollow, Quail Creek and Gunlock. See our guide to fishing near St. George.

Do I need fishing experience?

No. Trips suit beginners, families and experienced anglers, and expert instruction is included.
