Fishing outside the park

Capitol Reef National Park is known for its red rock cliffs and the Waterpocket Fold, not for fishing. Our guided trips for Capitol Reef visitors are on lakes in the surrounding region, outside the park. Check the National Park Service website for current rules about fishing inside the park.

Lakes that fit a Capitol Reef trip

Two of our lakes fit a Capitol Reef trip. They are very different days, so pick the one that matches what you want from the water.

Deep water Mackinaw hunting

Guided fishing at Fish Lake

A serious trout destination, with lake trout (Mackinaw), splake, rainbow and cutthroat. This is deep-water hunting for a fish that can weigh tens of pounds, in Utah’s high country. In winter the lake becomes an ice fishing destination.

  • Lake trout
  • Splake
  • Rainbow trout
  • Cutthroat trout
  • Other trout

Canyon striper adventure

Guided fishing at Lake Powell

The biggest variety in Southern Utah: striped bass, smallmouth and largemouth bass, walleye, crappie, catfish and bluegill. Best fished by boat. For Capitol Reef visitors it is a longer day, so ask us which fits your plans.

  • Striped bass
  • Smallmouth bass
  • Largemouth bass
  • Walleye
  • Crappie
  • Catfish

A fishing day between park days

Capitol Reef is one of the quieter national parks, and a guided fishing day fits that pace. Fish Lake is the trophy-hunting day, and Lake Powell is the canyon-variety day. Either one gives you a full day outdoors without the planning.

A guided trip with Henrie Outfitters is a full 8 hour day on the water with owner and guide James Henrie. All fishing gear and equipment and lunch are provided, start and end times are customized to fit your schedule, and you get expert instruction and local knowledge. Beginners, families and experienced anglers are all welcome.

Questions about fishing near Capitol Reef

Can you fish in Capitol Reef National Park?

This page covers guided trips on lakes in the region, not fishing inside the park. Check the National Park Service website for current rules.

What can I catch at Fish Lake?

Lake trout (Mackinaw), splake, rainbow trout, cutthroat trout and other trout.

Can I fish at Fish Lake in winter?

In winter, Fish Lake becomes an ice fishing destination. Contact us to ask about winter trips.
