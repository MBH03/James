What is included

  • Your boat and captain, owner and guide James Henrie
  • All rods, tackle, bait and lures
  • Properly fitted Coast Guard approved life jackets for adults and children
  • Bottled water and ice on the boat
  • Fish cleaning, bagged with ice, if you want to keep your catch
  • Hands-on instruction for beginners

What to bring

  • Your Utah fishing license
  • Weather appropriate clothes and warm layers
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Snacks or lunch for your group
  • A cooler to take your fish home in

Please leave dogs at home. Because of insurance requirements and open hooks in a small boat, we cannot accommodate dogs.

Deposit and payment

A 50% deposit secures your date. It is non-refundable but fully transferable to another date within 12 months. We accept cash, card and Zelle. The remaining balance is due the morning of your trip.

Cancellation and weather

Cancellations made 3 or more days in advance receive a full refund or a free reschedule. Cancellations within 3 days forfeit the deposit unless we are able to rebook your date.

We fish in rain or shine. If we must cancel because of lightning, high winds or unsafe conditions, you can choose a full refund or a free reschedule.

When to book

We recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance. June, July and early August are our busiest months, along with October for fall trophy season. Weekends book first, so weekdays have more availability.

Start times and meeting place

We meet you at the lake, not in town, to maximize your time fishing. Summer trips typically start at 7:00 AM and fall trips at 8:00 AM. We share the exact meeting location when you book. Depending on the season, we use different spots on each lake.

Half day trips

We specialize in full day charters. Because of the travel to our lakes, a full day gives you the most time on the water and the best fishing experience.

Groups and families

Every trip is a private charter. We host families, friends, corporate outings, bachelor parties and birthday celebrations. The maximum is 4 anglers per boat, and for larger groups we can coordinate multiple boats with other trusted local guides.

Kids, beginners and accessibility

Beginners are always welcome. Children are welcome with no minimum age, though we cannot take infants under 12 months because life jackets do not fit securely, and children under 13 wear a life jacket at all times in the boat. For guests with limited mobility, please call us. Many of our ramps are accessible and some are not, and we will recommend the best lake for your needs.

Our boat and how we fish

We fish from our private 18 foot Klamath Alaskan boat, designed for stability and safety on large mountain reservoirs. On our mountain trout lakes we troll for trophy trout with downriggers, lead core and flat lines, and all gear is provided.

If you keep fish, we clean them and bag them with ice at no extra charge. Keep fish within the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources limits for that lake. We also support catch and release for trophy fish.

Rates and policy questions

How much does a guided fishing trip cost?

Pricing is per boat, not per person. A full day is $1,050 for 1 to 2 anglers, $1,225 for 3 anglers and $1,400 for 4 anglers, which is the maximum per boat.

How long is a full day?

Up to 6 hours of fishing time. With travel, plan for about 8 hours, depending on where you are coming from.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A 50% deposit secures your date. It is non-refundable but fully transferable to another date within 12 months. We accept cash, card and Zelle, and the balance is due the morning of your trip.

What is your cancellation and weather policy?

Cancel 3 or more days ahead for a full refund or a free reschedule. Cancellations within 3 days forfeit the deposit unless we can rebook your date. We fish in rain or shine, and if we cancel for lightning, high winds or unsafe conditions you can choose a full refund or a free reschedule.

Is there a minimum age?

No minimum age, and children are welcome. We cannot take infants under 12 months because life jackets do not fit securely. Children under 13 must wear a life jacket at all times in the boat.

Can I bring a dog?

No. Because of insurance requirements and open hooks in a small boat, we cannot accommodate dogs.
